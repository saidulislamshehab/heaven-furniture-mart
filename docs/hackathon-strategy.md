# Heaven Furniture Mart — Hackathon Strategy

## Main Objective

Do not try to impress judges with the number of features.

Impress them with:

- Taste
- Visual storytelling
- Brand understanding
- Polish
- Performance
- Attention to detail

---

# The Winning Formula

Strong First Impression

- Clear Brand Story
- Exceptional Visual Direction
- Bespoke Differentiator
- Smooth Interaction
- Excellent Mobile Experience
- Strong Final CTA

  ***

# Priority Order

## Priority 1

Hero section

The first screen must immediately feel premium.

---

## Priority 2

Visual consistency

Every section must feel like part of the same brand.

---

## Priority 3

Bespoke customization

This is the strongest business differentiator.

Make this memorable.

---

## Priority 4

Photography

High-quality real furniture photography can dramatically improve the project.

---

## Priority 5

Motion

Use subtle motion to make the experience feel polished.

Do not overuse animation.

---

## Priority 6

Mobile

The mobile version should feel intentionally designed.

---

## Priority 7

Performance

The final website should remain fast.

---

# What Can Make the Project Stand Out

Potential premium features:

- Cinematic hero image reveal
- Elegant typography animation
- Interactive bespoke process
- Editorial image gallery
- Material texture transitions
- Scroll-based storytelling
- Subtle image parallax
- Smooth page transitions

Only implement features that improve the story.

---

# Final Judge Experience

When judges open the website:

First 3 seconds:

"Wow, this looks premium."

First 10 seconds:

"I understand this is a luxury custom furniture brand."

After scrolling:

"They clearly understand the brand and its customers."

At the end:

"This feels polished, intentional, and ready for a real business."
