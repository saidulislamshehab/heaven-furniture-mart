# Heaven Furniture Mart — Landing Page Structure

## Page Strategy

The landing page should tell a visual story.

The visitor should move through this journey:

Luxury First Impression
↓
Understand the Brand
↓
Explore the Collections
↓
Understand Customization
↓
Build Trust
↓
See Craftsmanship
↓
Take Action

---

# Section 1 — Navigation

Purpose:

Provide simple navigation without distracting from the experience.

Suggested navigation items:

- Home
- Collections
- Bespoke
- Our Story
- Contact

Primary CTA:

Request a Consultation

Navigation should be:

- Minimal
- Elegant
- Sticky after scroll if appropriate
- Transparent or visually integrated with hero

---

# Section 2 — Hero

Purpose:

Create an immediate luxury impression.

The hero should include:

- High-quality furniture or interior image
- Strong headline
- Short supporting text
- One primary CTA

Suggested headline:

Furniture, Crafted Around You.

Alternative:

Designed for Your Space.
Crafted for Your Life.

Supporting text:

Bespoke furniture and interior styling designed around your space, lifestyle, and personal taste.

Primary CTA:

Start Your Design Journey

The hero should feel cinematic and immersive.

Avoid:

- Too much text
- Multiple buttons
- Product grids
- Discount messaging

---

# Section 3 — Brand Introduction

Purpose:

Explain who Heaven Furniture Mart is.

Content should communicate:

- Based in Chattogram
- Bespoke furniture
- Interior styling
- Custom craftsmanship

Use a large editorial layout.

Possible structure:

Large heading on one side.

Short brand story on the other.

Add a beautiful showroom or furniture image.

---

# Section 4 — Collections

Purpose:

Show what the company creates.

Categories:

- Living
- Bedroom
- Dining
- Office
- Bespoke

Do not create a traditional product catalog.

Use:

- Large photography
- Editorial image cards
- Minimal text

Each category should feel like part of a luxury magazine spread.

---

# Section 5 — Bespoke Experience

Purpose:

Highlight the strongest differentiator.

This should be one of the most visually important sections.

Possible headline:

Made Around You.

Explain that furniture is designed around:

- Space
- Lifestyle
- Taste
- Requirements

Show a process:

01 — Share Your Vision

02 — Design Consultation

03 — Crafted by Experts

04 — Delivered to Your Space

This section should communicate the transformation from idea to finished furniture.

---

# Section 6 — Why Choose Heaven

Purpose:

Build trust.

Trust points:

- Free Design Consultation
- Fully Bespoke
- Premium Materials
- Skilled Craftsmanship
- Delivery & Installation
- Trusted by Hundreds

Possible visual approach:

Minimal icon grid.

Avoid generic feature cards.

Use elegant typography and subtle visual elements.

---

# Section 7 — Craftsmanship / Visual Story

Purpose:

Show the quality of the work.

Use:

- Furniture close-ups
- Wood textures
- Skilled craftsmanship
- Showroom images
- Interior lifestyle photography

This section should rely heavily on visuals.

Minimal text.

---

# Section 8 — Social Proof

Purpose:

Increase credibility.

Use:

- Managing Director quote
- Showroom imagery
- Trust statement

Quote:

"At Heaven Furniture Mart, we believe furniture is more than just function; it is a reflection of lifestyle, taste, and comfort."

Supporting statement:

Trusted by hundreds of happy homeowners.

---

# Section 9 — Brand Milestones

Purpose:

Show brand history and credibility.

Timeline:

2020 — Founded

2021 — Agrabad showroom opened

2024–2025 — Exhibited at International Furniture Fair

2025 — Chamber of Commerce membership

2026 — Nationwide BFIOA recognition

Keep this section visually minimal.

---

# Section 10 — Final CTA

Purpose:

Convert the visitor.

Suggested heading:

Let's Create Something Made for You.

Supporting text:

Tell us about your space, your ideas, and your vision. Our team will help bring it to life.

Primary CTA:

Request a Consultation

Secondary contact option:

WhatsApp Us

The primary CTA should remain visually dominant.

---

# Section 11 — Footer

Include:

- Heaven Furniture Mart
- Address
- Phone
- Email
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube

Keep the footer premium and minimal.
