# Heaven Furniture Mart — Design System

## Design Direction

The visual system should communicate:

- Luxury
- Warmth
- Craftsmanship
- Sophistication
- Timelessness
- Interior design expertise

The design should feel closer to a premium architecture or interior design studio than an ecommerce platform.

---

# Color System

## Primary Dark

Deep Charcoal Teal

Suggested:

#1F2E2D

Usage:

- Dark sections
- Navigation
- Footer
- Large visual backgrounds
- Premium contrast areas

---

## Primary Background

Warm Ivory

Suggested:

#F5F1E8

Usage:

- Main background
- Content sections
- Large whitespace areas

---

## Primary Text

Deep Brown

Suggested:

#2B211C

Usage:

- Headings
- Primary text
- Editorial typography

---

## Accent

Muted Gold / Brass

Suggested:

#B08A45

Usage:

- Small details
- Lines
- Icons
- Highlights
- Buttons

Important:

Do not overuse gold.

Gold should feel like a subtle luxury detail.

---

## Material Accent

Natural Wood Tan

Suggested:

#B98A5B

Usage:

- Supporting accents
- Material-related elements
- Decorative details

---

# Typography

## Headings

Use an elegant editorial serif font.

Recommended options:

- Cormorant Garamond
- Playfair Display
- DM Serif Display

Preferred:

Cormorant Garamond

Characteristics:

- Large
- Elegant
- Editorial
- Spacious
- High contrast

---

## Body Text

Use a modern clean sans-serif.

Recommended options:

- Manrope
- Inter
- Plus Jakarta Sans

Preferred:

Manrope

---

# Typography Hierarchy

## Hero Heading

Very large.

Desktop:

72px–110px

Mobile:

48px–64px

Use serif typography.

---

## Section Heading

Desktop:

48px–72px

Mobile:

36px–48px

Use serif typography.

---

## Body Text

Desktop:

16px–18px

Line height:

1.6–1.8

Keep paragraphs short.

---

# Spacing

The website should feel spacious.

Avoid tight layouts.

Recommended section spacing:

Desktop:

120px–180px

Tablet:

90px–120px

Mobile:

64px–96px

---

# Border Radius

Avoid excessive rounded cards.

Use:

- Small radius
- Sharp editorial edges
- Minimal rounded corners

Recommended:

8px–16px maximum

Large hero images can use subtle rounding if necessary.

---

# Shadows

Avoid heavy shadows.

Use:

- Very subtle shadows
- Soft depth
- Natural elevation

Luxury design should not look like SaaS UI.

---

# Buttons

Buttons should feel refined and intentional.

Primary button:

Dark background
Light text

Secondary button:

Transparent or light background
Subtle border

Avoid:

- Oversized pill buttons
- Excessive rounded corners
- Bright gradients
- Multiple competing CTA styles

---

# Layout Philosophy

Use:

- Large images
- Asymmetrical layouts
- Editorial grids
- Generous whitespace
- Text and image balance
- Full-width visual moments

Avoid:

- Repetitive card grids
- Dashboard-like layouts
- Generic landing page patterns

---

# Decorative Elements

Allowed:

- Thin lines
- Small gold accents
- Large typography
- Material textures
- Architectural grid lines
- Subtle image overlays

Avoid:

- Random blobs
- Excessive gradients
- Neon effects
- Glassmorphism
- Excessive icons
