# Heaven Furniture Mart — Project Context

## Project Overview

This project is a hackathon landing page for Heaven Furniture Mart.

The goal is NOT to build a full ecommerce website.

The goal is to design and develop a high-end, conversion-focused landing page that makes Heaven Furniture Mart feel like a premium luxury furniture and interior design studio.

The landing page should immediately communicate:

- Who Heaven Furniture Mart is
- What they create
- Why they are different
- Why customers should trust them
- What action the visitor should take

The final experience should feel premium, editorial, spacious, warm, and visually impressive.

# Primary Design Philosophy

The website should use storytelling instead of product listing.

Do not create a traditional ecommerce experience.

Avoid:

- Large product grids
- Product prices everywhere
- Discount banners
- Too many buttons
- Too many cards
- Crowded layouts
- Excessive text
- Loud colors

Instead focus on:

- Large photography
- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Editorial layouts
- Visual storytelling
- Craftsmanship
- Material textures
- Bespoke customization
- Emotional connection

---

# Primary Goal

The primary conversion goal is:

# Important Implementation Rules

When building this project:

- Do not redesign the project into a generic ecommerce website.
- Do not add unnecessary pages.
- Do not create a large product catalog.
- Do not overcrowd sections.
- Do not use excessive cards.
- Do not use random gradients.
- Do not use loud colors.
- Do not use generic stock-style layouts.
- Do not overuse animations.
- Do not sacrifice performance for visual effects.

Every design and development decision should support the feeling of:

> Luxury + Craftsmanship + Customization + Trust
